# Code of Conduct

This project follows the spirit of the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless
of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level
of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion,
or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and assume good intent.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the people who use the software, including the candidates whose
  resumes it processes.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks.
- Publishing others' private information, including any real resume data.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome attention.

## Enforcement

Report problems privately to the maintainers. Maintainers may remove comments, commits or
contributions that break this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors who
repeatedly do so. Reports are handled confidentially.
